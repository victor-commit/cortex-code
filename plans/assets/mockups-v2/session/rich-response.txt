   Three strategies fit here. The table ranks them by throughput and memory cost,
   then the sketch shows the bounded async variant.

   Strategy        Throughput   Memory   Verdict
   ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   async tasks     high         low      ✓ scales cleanly
   thread pool     medium       high     ● memory heavy
   process fork    low          high     ✗ avoid at scale
