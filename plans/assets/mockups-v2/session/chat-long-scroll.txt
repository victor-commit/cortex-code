 › where do refresh tokens get persisted?  · 26m ago

   Cortex

   They live in the sessions table, one row per device. TokenService writes them through
   store::persist() at issue time and rotates the row in place on every refresh, so a device
   never holds two live tokens at once.

 › why keep the old token around for five minutes after rotation?  · 9m ago

   Cortex

   The grace window absorbs racing requests: a client that fired two calls in parallel can
   still present the outgoing token a moment after the refresh landed. Within that window
   enforce() accepts the old token for reads but never for writes, which keeps the race
   harmless without weakening rotation.

   ● In short  rotation is immediate for writes; reads get a five-minute grace window.
